Research & Academic Work

Undergraduate research focused on computer vision, machine learning, and healthcare screening.

Enhancing VIA Screening for Cervical Cancer: A Comprehensive System Integrating Image Processing, Risk Factors, and Follow-up Facilitation

This research explored the use of computer vision and machine learning for Visual Inspection with Acetic Acid (VIA) screening. The work examined how medical image processing, risk-factor analysis, and data-driven methods could support screening workflows in a research-focused setting.

The study investigated methods including VGG16, ResNet50, YOLO, Random Forest, and related machine learning and computer vision techniques, with attention to medical image analysis, healthcare analytics, and the broader role of computational methods in health-related problems.
