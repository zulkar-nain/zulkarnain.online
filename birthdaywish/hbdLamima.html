A note for you

Written with unreasonable amounts of affection.

Dear Lamima,

Happy birthday to the love of my life. Somehow, we have been doing this beautiful, chaotic, occasionally ridiculous thing called “us” for about ten years now. That is a whole decade of memories, conversations, arguments about absolutely nothing, laughter at the wrong time, and a story I would still choose again every single day.

You have been my favourite person to talk to, annoy, miss, celebrate, and come home to. Thank you for being patient with me, for making ordinary moments feel special, and for bringing so much warmth into my life. You are brilliant, deeply loved, and still somehow willing to put up with me, which is honestly one of your most impressive achievements.

I hope this year gives you the happiness you deserve, the peace you need, and many reasons to smile until your cheeks hurt. I hope we keep collecting little adventures, making ridiculous memories, and finding our way back to each other through every chapter ahead.

I love you more than this page has pixels to explain.
Always yours,
Zulkar
